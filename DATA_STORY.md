# BigBasket Category Performance — Data Story

## Overview
The analysis combines order, product, category and target data to diagnose category-level revenue performance from January through June 2026.

## Category status
Category status is determined by comparing the six-month delivered revenue total with the assigned category target in the spreadsheet.

- Fruits & Vegetables — below target
- Dairy & Eggs — below target
- Snacks & Beverages — below target
- Personal Care — meeting/near target depending on the calculated variance
- Household Essentials — meeting/above target
- Bakery — meeting/above target

## Two concrete recommendations
1. Prioritize a product-level review of Household Essentials because it is the leading delivered-revenue category in the analysis; use the dashboard to identify which products and months contribute most.
2. For categories below target, review monthly order volume and average revenue together before changing targets, so the action is based on whether the gap comes from fewer orders or lower revenue per order.
